Changed today · 29 August 2026

  • Western Cape build (C) added, in both review and field-test form. Location anchor is Swellendam; hospitals are Swellendam Provincial (18 Drostdy Street) and Otto du Plessis in Bredasdorp, both government facilities, with Mediclinic Worcester for eye care.
  • The symptom check now opens with “Are you the victim?” — the answer decides whether the checklist asks what you feel or what you see, and sets the wording of every later question.
  • First aid 1 is titled To Do: and no longer tells anyone not to wait for an identification; the ID prompt now leads with “#1 priority is to get to the hospital, but if you can ID the snake, it will help your treatment.”
  • The swelling check carries an illustration of the marked limb, between the title and the instruction.

Changed 2 August 2026

  • Testkit build added for field testing: real camera capture, real GPS, distances computed from the live fix, working Google Maps directions links, and a full-screen phone layout with the inspector hidden. The design-review build is unchanged.
  • Region moved from Eswatini to the Mpumalanga lowveld, South Africa: Mbombela as the shared location, Rob Ferreira and Themba hospitals, Mediclinic Nelspruit for eye care, SA dialling codes and local place names in the picker. The module’s clinical fields still cite the Eswatini 2021 guidelines — that citation needs a South African replacement before the pilot.
  • “Pick a different spot” now opens WhatsApp’s own location picker — map with a draggable pin, search, and nearby places. The bot cannot render a pannable map inside a message, so the sheet is the client’s; either route returns one inbound location message.
  • The no photo → hospital variant is consolidated into the main prototype and retired. Its hospital instruction and keep-still first aid are now First aid 1: the turn that fires the moment a bite is reported, before any identification, because treatment does not wait for a species.
  • First aid 1 is CMS content keyed by species — an unidentified bite takes the generic entry, an identified one takes its species entry. A species with no First aid 1 content produces no turn at all; the flow continues straight to care rather than showing filler. (Southern African Python is wired that way in the prototype.)
  • New entry point: attaching a photo from the + button starts the flow cold. The bot confirms it is a snake, asks for location while it analyses, and identifies the species — then asks what happened: someone bitten, spat in the eye, or just more about the snake. A bite routes straight into first aid and the antivenom list for that species.

Changed 1 August 2026

  • Added the no photo → hospital variant, for comparison against the main prototype.
  • On the ID-a-snake path, the third option is now “ID by description” leading straight into the adaptive key. The bite path still defers, because that slot has to carry the antivenom escape.
  • Redrew the hood question’s two illustrations from different viewpoints, so the lifted snake no longer reads as hooded.

Changed 31 July 2026

  • The photo request now comes first. Location is asked once, while the image is being analysed, and never asked again.
  • The ID prompt is three options: take a photo, use an existing photo, or no photo — the snake is gone.
  • A non-venomous identification with a reported bite now runs a symptom screen. Any sign of envenoming overrides the identification and routes to a hospital; none routes to wound care.
  • Venom status is stated plainly — “Venomous.”, not “Likely venomous.” Doubt belongs to the identification, so it sits on the ID line and the match confidence.
  • Photo-match confidences reworked as one distribution (86 / 9 / 3 / 2%) rather than four separate scores.
  • Version A, the triage-first variant, has been dropped. The spec now covers this flow only.
