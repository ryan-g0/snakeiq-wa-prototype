Changed in D · 21 September 2026

  • A standing Menu, reachable from any card or by typing “menu”. Before arrival it offers transport, first aid, the bite record and an ID; after check-in it switches to the review set. “Bite information” is addressable by name the same way.
  • The bite path is paced: time of bite is asked first, then first aid, then “Plan ahead”, each waiting for an acknowledgement instead of stacking three cards at once. Hospital selection is one card.
  • Consent before the hand-off — nothing is sent to a hospital until the user says yes, and a refusal is honoured in every later claim the bot makes.
  • No antivenom product is named anywhere: which one to give is a decision made at the facility.
  • Cancelling splits in two — calling off the trip keeps the case open, “there was no bite” closes it — and neither ends in a dead end.
  • “ID by description” is offered on the bite path again, and always returns at least three species to choose between.
